Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

PickUpPushUp ("the App") is built privacy-first. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising. We collect the minimum data needed for the App to function.

What we collect

On your device only:

  • Screen-time usage data for the apps you choose to monitor (via Apple's Family Controls framework — Apple never shares the underlying app names with us)
  • Exercise log entries you create
  • App settings (selected exercise, ratio, monitored apps)

This data lives on your iPhone and in your private iCloud storage, which is end-to-end encrypted by Apple. We have no servers that receive or process it.

Anonymous purchase data (via RevenueCat):

When you subscribe to Analytics Premium, RevenueCat handles the purchase. RevenueCat receives an anonymous user ID, your purchase status, country code, and device platform. They do not receive your name, email, Apple ID, or any other identifying information. See RevenueCat's privacy policy for details.

Friends data (via iCloud):

If you create a profile to use the friends feature, your chosen display name, username, current streak, today's debt count, and total exercises completed are stored in your iCloud private database, shared only with users you accept friend requests from. You can delete this at any time in Settings → Reset All Data.

What we do not collect

  • No email addresses
  • No third-party tracking or analytics SDKs
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No location data
  • No contacts or photos
  • No identifiers linked to your real identity

Children's privacy

PickUpPushUp does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. The app is rated 4+ on the App Store.

Subscription billing

Subscriptions for Analytics Premium are managed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment information. To manage or cancel a subscription, open iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Your rights

You can reset all your local data at any time via Settings → Reset All Data. To delete your friends-feature profile from iCloud, tap that same reset button.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date at the top.
